Do I need to approve posts before they go live?

Yes, by default every post waits in your calendar for a look before it publishes. You can also switch a workspace to full autopilot once you trust the voice.

What if Framlin doesn't sound like me?

Every draft is editable in place. The more you tweak, the closer the next batch gets to your voice — Framlin learns from your edits, not just your first URL.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no lock-in. Cancel from your account settings and you'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

Which platforms are supported?

Instagram, Facebook, and X today. Small team plans can post to all connected channels at once.
